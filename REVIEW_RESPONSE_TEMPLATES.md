# BoroPro Review Response Templates

## Overview

This document contains professional, empathetic review response templates for both iOS App Store and Google Play Store. Use these templates to respond to user reviews, address concerns, and build community engagement.

**Key Principles:**
- Be professional and empathetic
- Address specific concerns mentioned in reviews
- Offer solutions or next steps
- Keep responses concise (100-300 characters for iOS, 500 characters for Android)
- Thank users for feedback
- Encourage continued use and communication

---

## Response Guidelines

### Do's ✓
- Be professional and courteous
- Thank the user for their feedback
- Address specific points mentioned in the review
- Offer solutions or next steps
- Provide contact information for support
- Keep responses concise
- Use proper grammar and spelling
- Show genuine appreciation
- Acknowledge valid concerns
- Offer to help resolve issues

### Don'ts ✗
- Be defensive or argumentative
- Ignore negative feedback
- Make excuses
- Promise features you can't deliver
- Use generic responses that don't address the review
- Be sarcastic or dismissive
- Argue with the user
- Share personal information
- Make false claims
- Ignore repeated complaints

---

## Response Strategy

### For Positive Reviews (5-4 stars)
1. Thank the user
2. Mention specific features they praised
3. Encourage continued use
4. Invite feedback for improvements

### For Mixed Reviews (3 stars)
1. Thank the user
2. Acknowledge what's working
3. Ask for specific feedback
4. Offer support contact

### For Negative Reviews (2-1 stars)
1. Apologize sincerely
2. Ask for specific details
3. Offer immediate help
4. Provide support contact
5. Show commitment to improvement

## Monitoring & Follow-up

### Review Monitoring Schedule
- Check reviews daily for first week after update
- Check reviews 2-3 times per week normally
- Respond to critical issues within 24 hours
- Respond to other reviews within 3-5 days

### Follow-up Process
1. Respond to review
2. If user contacts support, follow up
3. If issue is resolved, ask for review update
4. Track common issues for product improvements
