### Full Description (4000 characters max)

BoroPro is the essential reference tool for borosilicate glassblowers. Whether you're working in a professional studio or perfecting your craft, BoroPro provides precise calculations, detailed logging, and scientific references—all optimized for studio environments.

**REHEAT CALCULATOR**
Calculate exact reheat times for your glass pieces with precision. Input your glass diameter, type, thickness, starting temperature, and kiln temperature to get accurate time estimates. Supports multiple glass types including borosilicate, soda-lime, and specialty compositions. Save your calculations for future reference.

**KILN LOGGING & TRACKING**
Document every firing with detailed logs. Record piece descriptions, glass types, color data, annealing profiles, and temperature cycles. Track kiln performance over time and identify patterns in your work. Beautiful color visualization shows exactly how your pieces appear after firing.

**GLASS SCIENCE REFERENCE**
Access comprehensive information on borosilicate glass composition, thermal properties, annealing cycles, and working temperatures. Understand the science behind your craft with detailed explanations of glass structure, thermal shock prevention, and color behavior.

**FIRING TRACKER**
Monitor annealing cycles with precision. Create custom firing profiles with multiple phases, set target temperatures and heating rates, and track your progress through each phase. Real-time monitoring helps ensure consistent, professional results.

**COLOR LIBRARY**
Reference glass colors with precise color data. View color samples, compare hues, and understand how colors appear at different temperatures. Includes transmission data and spectral information for advanced color work.

**STUDIO-OPTIMIZED DESIGN**
- Dark theme reduces eye strain in bright studio environments
- Large touch targets work perfectly with gloved hands
- Offline mode works without internet connection
- Fast calculations for quick reference during work
- Professional, minimal interface focuses on essential information

**OFFLINE SUPPORT**
Access all tools and saved data even without internet connection. Perfect for studios without reliable connectivity.
