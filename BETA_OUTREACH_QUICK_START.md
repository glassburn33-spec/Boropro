## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Find Creators (Week 1)
1. Search YouTube for "glassblowing" + related terms
2. Look for channels with 1K-500K subscribers
3. Watch 2-3 videos to understand their content
4. Find their contact email (usually in "About" section or contact form)
5. Create a spreadsheet with: Name, Channel URL, Email, Status

**Target:** 40-50 creators to reach 12 beta testers

### Step 2: Send Outreach Emails (Week 1-2)
1. Personalize each email with creator's name and specific video reference
2. Use one of the templates above
3. Send 5-10 emails per day (spread throughout the day)
4. Track responses in your spreadsheet
5. Update status: "Contacted" → "Responded" → "Accepted"

**Expected response rate:** 20-30%

### Step 3: Follow-up (Week 2-3)
1. After 7 days, send follow-up to non-responders
2. After 14 days, send final follow-up
3. After 21 days, move to next creator if no response
4. Update spreadsheet with follow-up dates

**Expected acceptance rate:** 70-80% of responders

### Step 4: Send Beta Access (Week 3)
1. Send beta access link via email
2. Include simple setup instructions
3. Provide contact info for support questions
4. Set clear timeline for testing and reviews

### Step 5: Check-in (Week 4)
1. Send mid-testing check-in email
2. Ask for early feedback
3. Offer support if they have questions
4. Keep engagement high

### Step 6: Review Reminder (Week 5)
1. Send reminder 1 week before deadline
2. Provide review format suggestions
3. Make it easy to submit review
4. Thank them for participation

### Step 7: Thank You & Follow-up (Week 6)
1. Thank each beta tester personally
2. Share their review on your channels
3. Maintain relationship for future updates
4. Ask for ongoing feedback

## Tips for Success

### 1. Personalization is Key
- Use their name
- Reference specific videos
- Show you watch their content
- Make it about them, not just your app

### 2. Timing Matters
- Send emails Tuesday-Thursday
- Send mid-morning or early afternoon
- Avoid Mondays and Fridays
- Space out emails (5-10 per day)

### 3. Make It Easy to Say Yes
- Clear value proposition
- Simple next steps
- Low time commitment
- High reward

### 4. Follow-up is Important
- 20-30% respond to first email
- 50-60% respond to second email
- 70-80% respond to third email
- Don't give up too early

### 5. Build Relationships
- Thank them personally
- Share their reviews
- Stay in touch
- Ask for ongoing feedback

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
- Send generic emails to everyone
- Use "Hey there" instead of their name
- Make it too long
- Bury the ask
- Forget to follow up
- Send at odd times
- Spam them
- Make unrealistic promises

✓ **Do:**
- Personalize each email
- Use their name
- Keep it short and clear
- Lead with the value
- Follow up consistently
- Send during business hours
- Respect their time
- Be honest about what you need

## Creator Search Tips

### Where to Find Creators
1. **YouTube Search:** "glassblowing," "glass art," "borosilicate glass," "kiln," "annealing"
2. **YouTube Recommendations:** Check related channels on popular videos
3. **Social Media:** Search hashtags like #glassblowing, #glassart, #borosilicate
4. **Glass Communities:** Check forums, Reddit, Facebook groups
5. **Glassblowing Conferences:** Look for presenters and teachers

### How to Find Their Email
1. YouTube channel "About" section
2. Channel website (usually linked in About)
3. Email in video descriptions
4. Contact form on their website
5. Social media profiles
6. LinkedIn profile
7. Last resort: Comment asking for contact info

### Creator Tier Strategy

**Tier 1 (100K+ subscribers):** 3-4 creators
- High reach
- More influence
- Harder to reach
- Better for partnerships

**Tier 2 (10K-100K subscribers):** 5-6 creators
- Good engagement
- Responsive
- Easier to reach
- Good for reviews

**Tier 3 (1K-10K subscribers):** 3-4 creators
- Growing audience
- Very responsive
- Easy to reach
- Good for community
